# Contributing

Open Antarctica is intended to stay reproducible, open, and careful about data provenance.

## Ground rules

- Do not commit large geospatial datasets or generated tile caches.
- Keep region footprints and processing steps reproducible.
- Record source datasets, acquisition dates, CRS, resolution, and attribution requirements.
- Keep raw scientific/source layers distinct from any future visually reconstructed or AI-enhanced layers.
- Avoid introducing commercial or restricted data as a required dependency for the open viewer.

For the initial phase, development is focused on the Ferrar Glacier v0.0.1 proof of concept.
